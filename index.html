A girl from Darbhanga who finds beauty in small things, talks like she's known you forever, and writes so neatly you'd think the page came printed.

Hi, I'm Devanshi — a Class 7 student with a pen, a phone full of aesthetic photos, and a head full of sky-high dreams.

I love writing by hand — every page is a little piece of art. I love talking to people; conversations are kind of my superpower. I study hard, play Free Fire for fun, and I follow the trends that glow.
